# Vela Editorial Folio

The opening is a project-owned indigo watercolor on paper: one horizon, one
small sail, and sparse atmospheric marks. The canonical Vela sail orients the
reader; the painting is atmosphere and never a scientific graph.

The composition is deliberately photographic: one full-bleed plate, a quiet
edge-aligned masthead, and no added marketing bands. Controls use IBM Plex Mono
under SIL OFL 1.1. Other type roles use system Iowan Old Style, Baskerville,
Times New Roman, and the native sans stack, transferring no font bytes.

The page must preserve visible focus, 44px targets, reduced motion, forced
colors, print, and layouts at 320, 375, 414, and 768 CSS pixels. The authored
plate disappears in forced colors and print; orientation and links remain.
